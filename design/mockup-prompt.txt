Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: visual development reference for a browser-based Three.js low-poly cafe simulation MVP.
Primary request: create one polished landscape 16:9 gameplay mockup of a small cozy isometric cafe. It must look like an achievable real-time low-poly 3D game screenshot with a crisp flat HTML UI overlay, not a painting or complex cinematic render.
Scene: one small rectangular cafe as a floating cutaway diorama on a plain warm off-white background, two rear walls only, open front edges, no roof. The complete floor fits on screen with generous margin. Fixed orthographic isometric camera at about 45 degrees azimuth and 35 degrees elevation. Equal scale regardless of depth.
Layout: a straight wood service counter along the rear-left wall, a blocky espresso machine, register, small cup stack. A visible space behind counter contains one barista in sage apron. Three small square wooden tables with two simple chairs each in the open seating area. Clear generous walkways. A floor entrance gap at the near-right edge. Two customers queue at the register, one seated customer, one customer walking toward a table. Exactly three tables, six chairs, one barista and four customers. One simple potted plant in the far corner, one rear window, one small menu board. Modest props.
Style: chunky deliberately low-poly geometry, beveled boxes, low-sided cylinders, faceted leafy plants, simple small people made from capsule/box limbs and plain heads, no detailed faces. Matte flat colors, warm wood, cream walls, sage green accents, muted terracotta seating, dark coffee-brown details. Soft directional sunlight and restrained contact shadows. No photographic textures, no depth of field, no complex reflective materials.
Composition: cafe takes about 75 percent of image width, centered beneath slim top HUD and above compact bottom toolbar. Clean readable game view, no decorative frame or title screen.
UI text verbatim: top left small heading "CAFE" with sublabel "Day 1"; top right three tidy chips "$ 240", "Served 4", "Waiting 2". Bottom center a compact cream rounded toolbar with three buttons "Open cafe", "Pause", "1x". Small speech bubble with simple coffee cup icon over the first waiting customer; a small green progress bar over the espresso machine. UI uses dark brown clear sans-serif text, subtle borders, sage active button. Keep text minimal and exact.
Constraints: one screenshot only, no explanatory labels, no extra inset panels or swatches, no watermarks, no exterior town or street, no additional rooms. Prioritize buildable geometry, readable circulation, consistent camera, furniture scale and clean game UI.
